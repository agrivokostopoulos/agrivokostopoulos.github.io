Please find attached my paper and CV for the WEast 2026 Belgrade workshop.

The paper, 'Between Civilisation and Constitution: Greek Parliamentary Debates on Gold Standard Adoption, 1875–1885', reconstructs Greek parliamentary debates over gold standard adoption using newly digitised proceedings of the Hellenic Parliament. It asks why technically well-founded objections to gold adoption failed politically, and places Greek monetary reform in the wider setting of institutional change, parliamentary politics, and economic development in South-East Europe.

I am an MPhil/PhD candidate in Economic History at the London School of Economics, supervised by Albrecht Ritschl and Olivier Accominotti. I would also be grateful to be considered for any available junior researcher support.
